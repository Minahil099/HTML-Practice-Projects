Delicios Homemade Choclate Chip Cookies

these delecious choclate choip cookies are soft chewy and packed with rich choclate flavour.Perfect for any occasion and loved by all ages!

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups all purpose floor
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter,softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar,packed
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cup semi sweet choclate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Mix the flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Beat the butter, eggs, and vanilla extract until smooth.
  4. Add the dry ingredients and mix well.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop the dough onto a baking tray.
  7. Bake for 10–20 minutes until edges are golden brown.
  8. Let the cookies cool before serving.
